# Bracket

L-shaped NEMA 17 motor mount, aluminium 6061-T6. A base plate (bolted with 2 × M5 at x = ±20 mm) and an upright
plate of thickness `thickness`, width `width` and height `plateHeight` (mm) carrying the motor on its face.
Two triangular ribs sit at the side edges of the upright. The 22.5 mm pilot bore and 4 × M3 (31 mm pitch) are
centred on the motor axis. Load: 60 N radial at a 50 mm lever from the face, so the plate sees bending plus a
moment. Bounds: thickness 2–8, width 46–80, plateHeight 40–60.

Deflection is the motor-axis displacement in mm.
